// Pricing FAQ
QUESTIONS ANSWERED.
What counts as one conversion? ▼
One file in, one file out = one conversion. Batch jobs count each image individually. Resize-only and DPI-only operations also count as one conversion each.
How does PPP pricing work? ▼
We detect your country at checkout and apply a local price multiplier automatically. India pays ~15% of the USD price. Brazil ~25%. No coupon needed, no VPN tricks — just fair pricing for your market.
Can I cancel anytime? ▼
Yes. Cancel from your dashboard, no questions asked. You keep access until the end of your billing period. No cancellation fees.
Do unused conversions roll over? ▼
No. Monthly quota resets at the start of each billing period. We don't roll over unused conversions.
Is there a free trial for Pro? ▼
The free tier is effectively a permanent free trial with 500 conversions per month. No time limit. If you need more, upgrade to Pro anytime.
