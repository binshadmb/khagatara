Here’s a concise summary of the Google AdSense Terms of Service:

Key Points of Google AdSense Terms
1. You Must Follow Google’s Rules

By using Google AdSense, you agree to:

AdSense Terms of Service
AdSense Program Policies
Google Branding Guidelines

Breaking these rules can lead to account suspension or termination.

2. Account Requirements
You must be 18+ years old
Only one AdSense account is allowed per person/business
Google can request ID/address verification anytime
Google may approve or reject your site/app
3. Google Can Show Ads on Your Content

By joining AdSense, you allow Google to:

Display ads and search results on your website/app
Access, crawl, cache, and index your content
Refuse service to certain websites/apps

Apps may need extra approval and must follow Google software policies.

4. Google Can Change Services or Terms Anytime

Google may:

Add/remove features
Suspend services
Update policies

Most changes take effect after 30 days, but urgent/legal changes can apply immediately.

5. How Payments Work

You earn money from:

Valid ad clicks
Valid impressions
Other approved ad interactions

But:

Google only pays if your site fully complies with policies
Payments happen after reaching the payout threshold
Payments can be delayed during investigations

Google can:

Deduct invalid earnings
Reverse payments
Withhold money from suspicious traffic
Important: Invalid Activity Rules

Google strictly prohibits:

Clicking your own ads
Fake traffic
Bots or automated clicks
Paying users to click ads
Misleading users into clicking

If detected:

Earnings may be removed
Account may be banned permanently
Future monetization on Google products may be blocked
6. Suspension & Termination

Google may suspend or terminate accounts for:

Policy violations
Invalid traffic
Inactivity (6+ months)

If terminated for violations:

You may lose unpaid earnings
You cannot create another account

Appeals must usually be filed within 30 days.

7. Taxes
You are responsible for your own taxes
Google may withhold taxes if required by law
8. Google Can Run Experiments

Google may test ad layouts/features on your site without notice.

9. Intellectual Property
Google owns AdSense software and branding
You get limited permission to use Google branding
Reverse engineering or copying Google software is prohibited
10. Privacy Requirements

You must have a clear privacy policy explaining:

Cookies
Data collection
User consent
Device/location tracking

You may need user consent under laws like GDPR.

11. Confidentiality

You cannot publicly share confidential Google information such as:

Internal metrics
Beta features
Certain performance statistics
12. Legal Responsibility

You must protect Google from legal claims related to:

Your website/app content
Policy violations
Misuse of AdSense
13–14. No Guarantees & Limited Liability

Google does not guarantee:

Revenue
Ad availability
Specific performance

Services are provided “as is.”

Google’s financial liability is heavily limited.

15. Legal Terms
California law governs disputes
Cases must be handled in Santa Clara County, California
Terms survive even after account closure in some situations
Practical Takeaways for Publishers
Do:
Use original, policy-compliant content
Maintain good traffic quality
Keep privacy policies updated
Monitor invalid traffic carefully
Don’t:
Click your own ads
Buy fake traffic
Use misleading ad placements
Create multiple accounts
Biggest Risk Areas

The most common reasons people lose AdSense accounts are:

Invalid traffic/click fraud
Copyright or low-quality content
Misleading ad placement
Multiple account violations
Policy non-compliance
