When the prompt gets routed through fallback translators (MyMemory, Google Free), they translate the prompt itself instead of executing it — so instead of JSON back, you get a translated version of the instructions, which then fails parseJSON.

Why #0A0A0A over #000000: Pure black on pure white creates harsh halation on OLED screens — eyes perceive a slight blur. 
#0A0A0A is imperceptibly off-black but renders sharper on all screen types.

Gold primary:     #B07A10  (darker gold — readable on white)

Primary heading:  #0A0A0A  (near-black — crisper than pure #000)
